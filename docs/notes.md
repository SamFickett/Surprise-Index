## Surprise Measurements:

 - The current measure captures explicit related language, while some articles are describing events that may seem surprising contextually 
 without including any of the explicitly listed indicators. This results in that article, which is an inherently surprising story getting a 0
 for a surprise score.  

Ex:
 - Measure 1: Explicit Lexical
 - Measure 2: Semantic Contextual

 - Article Title: Trump signs order to rename Lake Ontario ‘Lake America’ amid Canada trade war
 - Article URL: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/27/trump-canada-lake-america-ontario.html

 - No explicit use of surprise phrases, the story itself is surprising
 - Recieved a surprise score of 0 with current implementation
